      📘 Chartink Scanner & Formula Syntax Guide

      1. Understanding Clause Sentences

      A scan clause defines a criteria that every scanned stock must satisfy. In our Chartink engine, each condition reads like plain English:

      Monthly Rsi( 14 ) Crossed above Number 45

      2. Candle Offsets (Past Bars)

      You can reference past historical candles using bar indexing:

      • [0] = Current latest candle
      • [-1] = 1 candle ago (previous session)
      • [-2] = 2 candles ago

      Example: [-1] Daily Close > [-2] Daily High scans for stocks whose yesterday's close exceeded day-before-yesterday's high.

      3. Math Expressions (±=)

      Click the ±= button on any clause line to add mathematical operations like multipliers (e.g. Volume > 2 * 20 SMA(Volume)) or custom percentage formulas.

      Choose from 28 authentic NSE universes including Nifty 500, Nifty 100, Bank Nifty, Gold/Silver ETFs, and My Watchlist.
